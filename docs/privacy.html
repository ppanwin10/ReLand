1. Scope

This policy explains how ReLand for iPhone and iPad and ReLand Host for Mac process information. ReLand is an open-source remote workspace that connects devices you control.

2. Information ReLand processes

To provide its features, ReLand can process the following information directly between your paired devices:

  • Mac screen or selected-window frames.
  • Pointer, keyboard, touch, and gesture input.
  • Terminal input, output, and session metadata.
  • Artifact metadata and files you choose to download.
  • File metadata and files from ReLand storage or Mac folders you explicitly approve.
  • Device names, private network addresses, pairing credentials, and connection state.

Apple defines collection as transmitting data off the device so the developer or a third party can retain or access it. ReLand does not collect the content above because Land AI LLC does not receive it.

3. Local storage and retention

  • Pairing keys are stored in Apple Keychain.
  • App settings and paired-device preferences remain local.
  • Host sessions are stored under ~/Library/Application Support/ReLand.
  • Downloaded previews can be cached locally on iPhone or iPad.
  • Approved Mac folders are represented by macOS security-scoped bookmarks.

ReLand retains local information until you remove a pairing, clear app cache/session storage, delete downloaded content, or uninstall the apps. ReLand Settings includes cleanup controls for app-owned cache and session storage.

4. Apple permissions

iPhone and iPad

  • Local Network: connect to ReLand Host.
  • Camera: scan a one-time pairing QR code.

Mac

  • Screen Recording: stream the display or selected app.
  • Accessibility: send pointer and keyboard input.

ReLand cannot approve these permissions for you. Permission choices can be changed in Apple system settings.

5. Network services and third parties

ReLand connects over a trusted local network or a private Tailscale network. If you choose Tailscale, Tailscale independently processes network and account information under its own privacy policy. ReLand does not inspect your Tailscale plan or billing information.

ReLand is distributed through Apple and GitHub. Apple and GitHub may process download, account, device, or standard web-log information under their own privacy policies. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no Land AI analytics or advertising scripts.

6. Security safeguards

  • Pairing requires physical access and a one-time QR code.
  • Pairing codes expire after ten minutes and are consumed once.
  • Transport uses Apple Network.framework TLS with pre-shared keys.
  • Public destination addresses are rejected.
  • Hidden paths, traversal, and symbolic links are rejected.
  • Mac folder access requires explicit approval.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep devices updated, protect access to the unlocked Mac, and never expose ReLand Host directly to the public internet.

7. Children

ReLand is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. ReLand does not create user accounts or collect personal information from children or adults.

8. Policy changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. App Store privacy disclosures will be updated when ReLand's data practices change.
